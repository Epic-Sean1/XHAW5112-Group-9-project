# Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena

## About the App

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a Flutter app that introduces users
to the arena's gaming packages and individual gaming experiences. It
also provides a way to estimate fees and find business information.

## Main Functions

-   **Home:** The main screen for navigating the app.
-   **Gaming Packages:** Browse the available gaming packages.
-   **Individual Experiences:** Explore experiences such as Virtual
    Reality, Racing Simulator, and Escape Room.
-   **Fees / Get a Quote:** Select the gaming packages and individual
    experiences you want. Choose **Request Quote** to display the quote
    based on your selections.
-   **More:** Open the About Us and Contact Us screens.

## How to Use

1.  Open the app using the provided Netlify website link.
2.  Use the bottom navigation to move between Home, Packages, Individual
    Experiences, Fees, and More.
3.  Open **Packages** or **Individual Experiences** to browse options.
4.  Open **Fees** to select the items you want included in your quote.
5.  Select **Request Quote** to view the calculated quote.
6.  Use **More** to read About Us information or view Contact Us
    details.

## Access

The app is published as a web app on Netlify.
